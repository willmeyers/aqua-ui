Props & Events
labelthe title-bar text (label, never title: that is the tooltip attribute)
statusadds the status bar with this text
metalthe brushed surface from Surfaces, unbroken under the title bar
sheetthe darker sheet fill
inactivethe unfocused state: silver gems, chrome sits back
toolbarputs the oval in the title bar; toolbar="hidden" starts with the strip away. toggleToolbar(show?) from script
no-controlsno traffic lights
traffic lightspurely aesthetic; hover and press states come from the artwork
toolbartoggledetail.shown; the oval is window chrome, which is why it stays when the strip slides away
aqua-toolbar · aqua-toolbar-item
smallthe icon-beside-label form
itemslabel, src, value, disabled; divider draws the dotted group rule, spacer pushes what follows right
commanddetail.value of the pressed item

  message: "Empty the Trash?",

});
